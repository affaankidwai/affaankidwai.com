For a long time, my photographs have lived in folders with names like
`Ranthambore-March` and `Bharatpur-Birds-2024`. Friends would ask to see
something and I'd send a Drive link, or pull out a phone, or promise to send
later and forget.

This site is the slow version of that promise. A place to keep the frames I
keep coming back to, written down in the only honest way I know — with the
long, slow story of how each one happened.

## What's here

- A growing **gallery** of photographs. Every frame is something I shot,
  on a trip I actually took, with light I had to wait for.
- **Field notes** — these blog entries — for the longer stories. The trips,
  the gear thoughts, the small decisions, the misses.
- An **about** page for the day job side of life: software, databases,
  systems thinking. Two halves that turn out to be more related than they
  look.

## What's coming

Travel. Cars. Music. Cards. Each will get its own corner when there's
enough material to deserve one. For now, wildlife is what has the strongest
gravity, and that's where the site begins.

> If you've found your way here through a tiger, a kingfisher, or by accident
> from somewhere on the internet — welcome. Stay as long as you like.

— Affaan
